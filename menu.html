Our Menu

Updated weekly. Driven by what's seasonal, local, and right.

Our menu changes every week based on what arrives from our partner farms in the Chilterns and Kent. Tonight's selection is current as of this week — we recommend calling ahead if you have a particular dish in mind.

All dishes are prepared in a kitchen that handles nuts, gluten, dairy, and shellfish. Please inform your server of any allergies before ordering.
